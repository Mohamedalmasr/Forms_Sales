Monthly Trust Reconciliation Checklist

Use one checklist per trust account per month. Attach this form to the full bank reconciliation package.

B. Month-End Cut-Off

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
All trust transactions for the month recorded.
All closed deals updated for trust movements.
Complete month-end bank statement obtained.

C. Trust Liability Listing

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
Trust liability listing prepared as of month-end.
File-level / client-level balances clearly shown.
Total trust liability calculated.
Unusual or long-standing balances identified and flagged for review.

D. Bank Reconciliation

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
Outstanding deposits listed and support attached.
Outstanding cheques / withdrawals listed and support attached.
Bank errors or other adjustments documented with explanations.
Reconciled bank balance calculated and documented on reconciliation worksheet.

E. Comparison – Reconciled Bank Balance vs. Trust Liability

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
Reconciled bank balance equals total trust liability listing.
Any differences identified, investigated, and corrected before month-end sign-off.

F. Reconciling Items Log

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
Reconciling items log updated for current month.
All reconciling items over two months old reviewed and followed up.
Any stale-dated, unexplained, or irregular items escalated to Licensed Manager for review.

G. Months With No Activity (If Applicable)

Complete this section only if there were no trust transactions during the month.

Checkpoint   Comments / Details
Confirmed there were no trust receipts or disbursements this month.
Trust account bank balance agrees to prior month and to trust liability listing.
Prior reconciling items (if any) still valid and explained.

H. Sign-Off & Filing

Preparer Declaration

I confirm that this monthly trust account reconciliation summary is complete and accurate and that it is supported by the underlying reconciliation package (including the bank reconciliation, trust liability listing, and reconciling items log).

Licensed Manager Review

I have reviewed the monthly trust account reconciliation and I am satisfied that it has been properly prepared and that the reconciled bank balance agrees to the total trust liability.
